Why This Matters

Elements invests in healthcare companies with proven products. The gap from proven to market-leading is commercial execution.

Elements backs small and mid-sized healthcare companies addressing high-priority needs — innovative care and reimbursement models, home-based care, payer/provider enablement, behavioral health, and underserved patient populations. In every one of those categories, clinical excellence does not automatically translate to commercial traction. Building the payer, provider, employer, and partnership motions required to scale revenue is a specific discipline, and it is what we do.
Parasol is a healthcare-heavy revenue firm. More than half of our active client bench is in healthcare — spanning value-based care, home-based care, behavioral health, dementia care, and benefits navigation. We embed with portfolio company leadership, own the revenue motion, and deliver measurable growth with aligned incentives. No VP of Sales hire, no 12-month ramp.
